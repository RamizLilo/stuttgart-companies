## Known gaps

- Employees: Mercedes-Benz only for 2023 (rounded), Porsche only for 2024 and 2025, Bosch for 2023 and 2025. Revenue per employee is therefore shown only where both values exist.
- Bosch states a margin, not an absolute EBIT, on the pages used.
- Mercedes-Benz figures for 2022 to 2025 are preliminary press figures, not the audited annual report.

## Why the profit figures cannot be compared directly

- Mercedes-Benz: adjusted EBIT (EBIT without special items)
- Porsche: operating result
- Bosch: EBIT margin from operations (a percentage)

The common measure is the **operating margin = profit / revenue** in percent. It is calculated for Mercedes-Benz and Porsche and reported for Bosch. The definitions still differ slightly, and the dashboard says so on its "Data & method" page.

Mercedes-Benz 2021 (revenue 133.9 bn, EBIT 16.0 bn) was found, but it is reported EBIT and not adjusted EBIT, so it is not mixed into the table.
